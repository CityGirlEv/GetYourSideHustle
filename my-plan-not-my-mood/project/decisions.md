# MY PLAN, NOT MY MOOD — ARCHITECTURAL DECISION LOG

---

## DECISION LOG

* **ADR-001: Focus Persona Alignment**:
  * *Decision*: Position Angela's launch messaging with a primary focus on women navigating midlife, career reinvention, and personal growth while offering unisex fit options.
  * *Rationale*: High purchasing power, massive emotional resonance, and highly underserved demographic in modern lifestyle apparel.

* **ADR-002: On-Demand Apparel Sourcing for MVP**:
  * *Decision*: Use premium Print-on-Demand (POD) partners (Bella+Canvas / AS Colour blanks) for initial launch validation.
  * *Rationale*: Eliminates up-front inventory risk and capital holding, allowing rapid slogan testing.

* **ADR-003: Signature Brand Magic Priority**:
  * *Decision*: Build the interactive "What's Your Mood Today?" tool directly into the homepage hero section for MVP launch.
  * *Rationale*: Differentiates the brand immediately from generic motivational merchandise and drives instant email capture.
