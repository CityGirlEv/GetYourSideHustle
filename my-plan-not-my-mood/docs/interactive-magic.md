# MY PLAN, NOT MY MOOD — SIGNATURE INTERACTIVE BRAND MAGIC SPECS

---

## 1. FEATURE 1: "WHAT'S YOUR MOOD TODAY?" INTERACTIVE TOOL

### Purpose
To transform static website traffic into an engaging daily brand ritual while driving email signups and contextual product conversion.

### User Flow
```
User visits Homepage / /mood
          |
          v
Selects Current Mood (Tired, Over It, Procrastinating, Anxious, Fired Up, Meh)
          |
          v
System Displays Personalized Response & Single Action Play
          |
          v
Option A: Download / Share "Plan Card" Graphic to Instagram / TikTok
Option B: Unlock 7-Day Free Challenge (Email Capture)
Option C: View Matching Apparel / Planner Product
```

### Mood Response Matrix

| Selected Mood | Brand Response Copy | Concrete Action Step | Product Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tired** | "Fatigue is real, but your long-term goal doesn't sleep. Take a deep breath." | *Drink 16oz of water and complete 15 minutes on your top priority.* | Heavyweight Relaxed Loungewear Tee |
| **Over It** | "Frustration means you care. Channel the energy into execution." | *Clear your desk and send the one email you've been delaying.* | 90-Day Execution Desk Pad |
| **Procrastinating** | "Scrolling won't fix it. The plan is waiting." | *Set a timer for 10 minutes and do the hardest task first.* | "Check the Box" Hoodie |
| **Anxious** | "Anxiety wants control. Action gives you control." | *Write down 3 things you can control today. Do number one.* | Habit Tracker Journal |
| **Fired Up** | "Energy is high. Lock it in with a system before the mood fades." | *Plan tomorrow's 3 non-negotiables right now.* | "MY PLAN, NOT MY MOOD" Classic Tee |

## 2. FEATURE 2: "WHAT WON TODAY?" DIGITAL RECEIPT GENERATOR

### Purpose
To create viral User-Generated Content (UGC) for Instagram Stories, TikTok, and community channels.

### Interface Spec
* **Input Form**:
  1. *Date*: [ Auto-populated ]
  2. *Today's Initial Mood*: [ Input Text / Pills ]
  3. *Today's Executed Plan*: [ Input Text / Bullet Points ]
  4. *What Won Today?*: [ Selection: `☐ MOOD` vs `☑ PLAN` ]
* **Output Card Generator**:
  * Renders a sleek 9:16 Instagram Story image preview featuring branded typography, checkmark graphic, and timestamp.
  * Direct "Save Image" or "Share to Instagram" button.

## 3. FEATURE 3: 7-DAY & 21-DAY CHALLENGE SYSTEM

### 7-Day Starter Challenge (Free Lead Magnet)
* **Theme**: "7 Days of Plan > Mood"
* **Structure**:
  * Day 1: The Non-Negotiables (Defining 1 daily play)
  * Day 2: Spotting Mood Traps
  * Day 3: The 10-Minute Rule
  * Day 4: Mid-Week Momentum
  * Day 5: Friction Reduction
  * Day 6: The "Did It Anyway" Victory
  * Day 7: Plan Receipts & Weekly Review
* **Delivery**: Automated daily email + printable PDF tracker.
