# MY PLAN, NOT MY MOOD — VISUAL BRAND IDENTITY SYSTEM

---

## 1. CREATIVE LANES & EARTHTONE PALETTE

### Soft Earthtone Aesthetic (Primary Brand Identity)
* **Aesthetic**: Warm, inviting, elevated lifestyle editorial. Replaces cold dark slates with rich organic earthtones that draw the customer in.
* **Primary Background**: Warm Linen / Cream (`#FAF8F5`).
* **Container Cards**: Crisp Warm Off-White / Sandstone (`#FFFFFF` & `#F3EFE6`).
* **Typography**: Deep Espresso (`#1F1917`) & Dark Charcoal (`#2D2623`).
* **Primary Accent**: Warm Terracotta Clay (`#C2410C`).
* **Secondary Accent**: Warm Forest Sage (`#047857`).
* **Highlight Accent**: Warm Amber (`#D97706`).
* **Border / Dividers**: Subtle Taupe (`#E7E2D7`).
