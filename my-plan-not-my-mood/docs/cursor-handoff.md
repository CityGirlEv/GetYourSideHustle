## 3. CORE DIRECTIVES FOR CURSOR IMPLEMENTATION

1. **No Mock Data Collusion**: Never display fabricated customer reviews or sales counters as real business statistics. Use `DEMO DATA` markers during staging.
2. **Interactive Magic First**: Prioritize the **"What's Your Mood Today?"** widget on the homepage before populating secondary store pages.
3. **Accessibility & Responsive First**: Mobile view (320px+) must feature zero horizontal scroll and crisp touch targets (min 44x44px).
