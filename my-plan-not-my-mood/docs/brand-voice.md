# MY PLAN, NOT MY MOOD — BRAND VOICE & COMMUNICATIONS GUIDE

---

## 1. VOICE ARCHITECTURE

The voice of **My Plan, Not My Mood** is that of an supportive, sharp, witty best friend or mentor who loves you too much to let you self-sabotage. 

### Core Attributes
* **Real over Refined**: Speak plainly. Avoid corporate buzzwords and fluffy wellness clichés.
* **Humor over Heavy**: Use humor to lighten the emotional burden of self-discipline.
* **Direct over Passive**: State the plan clearly. End with action.

---

## 2. VOICE IN PRACTICE ACROSS CHANNELS

### Website Copy
* *Header*: **Mood Said No. Plan Said Move.**
* *Subhead*: Your feelings get a vote, but your plan makes the executive decision. Shop the new collection of accountability wear & daily execution tools.

### Product Copy (Apparel)
* *Description*: "Made from 100% heavyweight combed cotton for when your mood wants to collapse on the couch, but your schedule says you have a empire to build. Fits oversized. Follow the plan."

### Social Media (Instagram / TikTok)
* *Caption*: "Woke up at 6:00 AM. Brain said '5 more minutes.' Mood said 'You're tired.' Plan said 'We do upper body today.' Guess who won? Check the receipt in story. #MyPlanNotMyMood #WhatWonToday"

### Email Marketing ("The Plan Check-in")
* *Subject*: Quick question: What's the mood today?
* *Body*: "Hey [Name], it's Wednesday. Mid-week fatigue is real. Hot flashes might be hitting. The inbox is overflowing. We know what the mood is saying right now. But what did the plan say on Sunday? Pick ONE item on your desk pad today and finish it. That's the play. - Angela"

### Customer Service / Order Confirmation
* *Confirmation Message*: "Receipt Confirmed. Your gear is officially in motion. While our warehouse team packs your order, take 30 seconds to write down tomorrow's top 3 plays. The plan starts now."

---

## 3. DO'S AND DON'TS

| DO | DON'T |
| :--- | :--- |
| Acknowledge that moods are real and valid. | Tell people to "just be positive!" or "smile more!" |
| Use sharp, funny, relatable everyday scenarios. | Use aggressive, toxic boot-camp shaming ("No excuses! Work until you bleed!"). |
| Focus on the single next concrete action. | Overwhelm customers with rigid 50-step systems. |
| Celebrate follow-through regardless of how small. | Dismiss small wins as insignificant. |
