Signing up for Medicare is not a single event; it is a structured timeline that spans seven months. This timeline, known as the **Initial Enrollment Period (IEP)**, is the primary window for most Americans to register for Medicare Part A and Part B when turning 65. Because your sign-up month determines exactly when your healthcare benefits begin, understanding this month-by-month progression is critical to preventing gaps in coverage and avoiding lifetime premium penalties.

This guide provides a detailed, chronological breakdown of the 7-month Medicare enrollment timeline. We will cover what to do each month, how to determine your coverage start date, and what steps to take to secure your health insurance transition.

For general background on enrollment windows, review our core directory page on medicare enrollment periods. If you are looking to build a step-by-step action plan, [see our checklist for 3 months before 65: your medicare action plan](/learning-center/medicare-at-65-action-plan).

## The 7-Month Initial Enrollment Period at a Glance

Your IEP is a seven-month window that begins three months before your 65th birthday month, includes your birthday month, and extends for three months after.

* **Months -3, -2, -1:** Early Enrollment. Coverage begins on the 1st day of your birthday month.
* **Month 0 (Birthday Month):** Enrollment. Coverage begins on the 1st day of the following month.
* **Months +1, +2, +3:** Late Enrollment. Coverage begins the month after you enroll.

Let's examine the actions and rules for each of these seven months.

---

## Month -3: The Starting Line (90 Days Before Your Birthday Month)

This is the first month you are legally allowed to submit your Medicare application. It represents the official start of your IEP.

### Key Focus: Establishing Your Account & Reviewing Basics
If you are not yet receiving Social Security benefits, you must actively sign up for Medicare. Your priority this month is to set up your online access portal and review the foundational rules.

* **Action Items:**
  1. Go to ssa.gov and create your secure "my Social Security" account.
  2. Gather your birth certificate or proof of citizenship, as Social Security may request verification during the online application.
  3. Read our comprehensive turning 65 medicare guide to align your timeline dates.

### When Coverage Begins
If you apply during Month -3, your Medicare Part A and Part B coverage will begin on the **first day of your birthday month**. For example, if your 65th birthday is October 18, your coverage will start on October 1.

---

## Month -2: Profiling & Coordination (60 Days Before Your Birthday Month)

Month -2 is dedicated to profiling your medical needs and coordinating with any employer health coverage you currently hold.

### Key Focus: Documenting Healthcare Needs & Work Rules
You must audit your current employer plan to see if you can delay Part B, and list your medications to find matching drug plans.

* **Action Items:**
  1. **Audit Employer Size:** If you or your spouse work for a company with 20 or more employees, contact your HR department to verify if your coverage is considered "creditable." If the company has fewer than 20 employees, you must enroll in Part B now to avoid coverage gaps.
  2. **Catalog Prescriptions:** Write down all current medications, dosages, and pharmacy preferences.
  3. **HSA Verification:** If you are contributing to an HSA, stop all contributions before the end of this month.

### When Coverage Begins
Enrolling during Month -2 secures your coverage start date for the **first day of your birthday month**.

---

## Month -1: The Target Apply Window (30 Days Before Your Birthday Month)

This is the most critical month of your enrollment timeline. Applying now ensures your Medicare card is in hand before your current coverage ends.

### Key Focus: Submitting the Online Application
Log in and complete your registration. If you want to delay drawing Social Security cash benefits, apply for "Medicare Benefits Only."

* **Action Items:**
  1. Log into ssa.gov and navigate to the Medicare enrollment link.
  2. Complete the online form. It takes less than 10 minutes. Print the application receipt.
  3. If coordinating with work, verify that your HR department has completed Form CMS-L564 for your records.

### When Coverage Begins
Applying in Month -1 guarantees your coverage starts on the **first day of your birthday month**.

---

## Month 0: Your Birthday Month

Happy 65th Birthday! If you have not applied yet, you are still within your birthday month window, but your start date will be slightly delayed.

### Key Focus: Finalizing Primary Enrollment & Choosing Secondary Plans
If you applied during the previous three months, your Red, White, and Blue card should have arrived. You will now use your Medicare Beneficiary Identifier (MBI) to buy supplemental plans.

* **Action Items:**
  1. Check the mail for your Medicare card.
  2. If traditional Medicare is your path, apply for a Medigap supplement (e.g., Plan G or N) and a standalone Part D prescription drug plan. Compare options in our guide on original medicare vs medicare advantage.
  3. If you haven't applied for Medicare yet, submit your SSA application immediately.

### When Coverage Begins
* **If you applied in Months -3, -2, or -1:** Your coverage is active on the **first day of this month**.
* **If you apply during this month (Month 0):** Your coverage will begin on the **first day of the following month** (e.g., if birthday is October 18 and you apply in October, coverage starts November 1).

---

## Month +1: The Late Window - Part 1 (1 Month After Your Birthday Month)

The birthday celebrations are over, and you have entered the latter half of your IEP. Enrolling now means your start date is delayed.

### Key Focus: Catching Up & Submitting Forms
If you missed your birthday window due to travel or delays, you must apply now to limit further coverage gaps.

* **Action Items:**
  1. Submit your online application via ssa.gov immediately.
  2. Note that you do not yet face late enrollment penalties, as you are still within your 7-month IEP.

### When Coverage Begins
If you apply during Month +1, your Medicare coverage will begin on the **first day of the following month**.

---

## Month +2: The Late Window - Part 2 (2 Months After Your Birthday Month)

Only two months remain in your Initial Enrollment Period. The window is closing rapidly.

### Key Focus: Securing Core Benefits
Enrolling during Month +2 is urgent if you do not have employer coverage.

* **Action Items:**
  1. Submit your application online.
  2. Review your budget to accommodate potential out-of-pocket medical bills during your coverage gap.

### When Coverage Begins
Your coverage will start on the **first day of the month following your enrollment**.

---

## Month +3: The Final Deadline (3 Months After Your Birthday Month)

This is the seventh and final month of your Initial Enrollment Period. Once this month ends, your IEP is closed.

### Key Focus: Immediate Enrollment Prior to Lock-Out
You must sign up before the final day of this month to avoid being locked out of Medicare until the next calendar year.

* **Action Items:**
  1. Submit your online application at ssa.gov before the last day of the month.
  2. If you miss this deadline, you must wait until the General Enrollment Period (January 1 - March 31) to sign up, and you may face a permanent Part B premium penalty.

### When Coverage Begins
Your coverage starts on the **first day of the month following your enrollment**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What happens if I miss the entire 7-month enrollment timeline?
If you miss your IEP, you are locked out of enrolling until the next General Enrollment Period (GEP), which runs from January 1 to March 31 each year. Your coverage will not begin until the month after you sign up, and you may be charged a permanent 10% premium penalty for each year you delayed.

### Does my coverage start immediately if I sign up during my birthday month?
No. If you enroll during your birthday month, your coverage starts on the first day of the following month. To have coverage active on your actual birthday, you must apply during the three months leading up to your birthday month.
