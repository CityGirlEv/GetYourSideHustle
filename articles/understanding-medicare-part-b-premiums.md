## What Part B covers

Medicare Part B helps cover outpatient care, physician services, preventive care, and durable medical equipment under Original Medicare.

## Standard premium vs. IRMAA

Most people pay the **standard Part B premium** set by CMS each year. Higher-income beneficiaries may pay an additional **Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount (IRMAA)** based on tax return information Social Security receives from the IRS.

## Where to verify amounts

Official premium tables are published by CMS and Medicare.gov. Amounts change annually, so always confirm the current year's figures from a government source.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this article enrollment advice?
No. Learning Center articles are educational only. They do not recommend specific plans, carriers, or enrollment actions.

### Does IRMAA apply to everyone on Medicare?
No. IRMAA is an additional amount on top of the standard Part B premium for beneficiaries whose income exceeds CMS thresholds.

### Where should I verify official Medicare rules?
Use [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov), 1-800-MEDICARE, or your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) for rules that apply to you.
